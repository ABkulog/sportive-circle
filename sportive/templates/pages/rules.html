Community rules

Athletic, not thirsty

The feed is for the sports side of campus life. Post your plans, PRs, questions, recaps and photos from the court, the trail and the gym. Keep it something your teammates, your club and your coach would be glad to see.

Post

  • Plans ("hiking Mt Si tomorrow, have a car, need 2"), progress and PRs, questions, game recaps and photos.
  • Tag the right sport, so it reaches the people who play it.
  • Gym and progress photos are welcome. Celebrate the work, not the body.

Don't post

  • Sexual or suggestive content, nudity, or photos taken to show off a body rather than a lift, a run or a game.
  • Rating, ranking or commenting on how people look. Ever.
  • Photos of other people who didn't agree to be posted.
  • Harassment, hate, threats, spam, or selling things.

If you see something

Tap Report on the post or the profile. Reports go to our team, and the person never finds out who reported them. We remove posts and suspend accounts that break these rules, and the Terms apply everywhere on Sportive Circle.

Sportive Circle is for UW students 18 and older.
