How it works

Play sports with other Huskies in 4 steps.

  1. 1

    Sign up with your UW email

    We email you a 6-digit code to prove you're a UW student.

  2. 2

    Find a game

    Home shows games for the sports you picked. Tap Join. We'll email you an hour before it starts.

  3. 3

    Or start your own

    Tap Create. Use Need players if you're playing right now, or New event to plan ahead.

  4. 4

    Join a club

    Open Clubs, follow the ones you like, and ask to join. An officer confirms new members.

Words you'll see

🤝 Props
A thank-you to a teammate after a game. "Good teammate!"
⬆️ Vouch
Telling the app a teammate is ready for harder games in that sport.
🔥 Rank
Your level in each sport: Casual → Intermediate → Competitive → Legend. Everyone starts at Casual. Ranks only matter for Intermediate and Competitive games.
🎟️ Tryout spot
A spot in a harder game for someone who hasn't ranked up yet.
🤝 +1
A friend you bring into a harder game with you.
⭐ Follow
See a club's updates and events. You're a member only after an officer confirms you.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. No ads, no fees.

Do I have to care about ranks?

No. Casual and All-levels games are open to everyone. Turn on 🧘 chill mode in Edit profile and nobody sees your ranks.

I'm already good. Do I have to start at the bottom?

No. Take a 🎟️ tryout spot in a harder game. If 3 players at that level vouch for you, you move up right away.

Can I play with a friend who isn't ranked?

Yes. In an Intermediate or Competitive game you've joined, tap "Bring a friend" to invite them as your +1.

How do I add friends?

Open Friends (the people icon at the top) and search for their name, or add people you've played with.

Will I get spammed with notifications?

No. You choose. In Profile → Edit profile → Notifications, pick which things show a number on the tab icons and which show in the "What's new" box on Home. The app never sends pop-ups; the only emails are your sign-up code and game reminders (which you can turn off).

Who can message me?

Friends, people you've played a game with, and officers of clubs you follow or asked to join. Tap ⋯ on anyone's profile to block or report them.

Who can see my email?

Only you and people you've played a game with.

Does the app track my location?

No. "Where am I?" on an event map asks your phone for your location once, uses it on your screen to show the distance, and never sends it to us. If it doesn't work, your browser is blocking location; the app tells you how to turn it on.

Can't make it to a game?

Tap Leave on the game so someone else gets your spot. Hosts: tap Cancel event and we'll email everyone.

Forgot your password?

Tap "Forgot password?" on the login page. We'll email you a code to set a new one.

How do I change my photo, turn off emails, or delete my account?

Go to Profile → Edit profile. Everything is there.

Is this run by UW?

No. It's a student project built for Huskies. It isn't an official University of Washington service. See Privacy and Terms.

Show up, cheer on beginners, have fun. Go Dawgs! 🐾
