How it works

Sportive Circle is where UW students find people to play with: pickup games, every UW sports club, and the places to play, in one app. Only students with a verified UW email can sign up.

Who's on it

  • Only UW students. Every account is checked with a UW email code.
  • Clubs with a ✓ are run here by their officers, checked by our team. The rest are listed from UW's own club pages until an officer claims them.
  • You choose what's private. Games can be private (invite or password), and clubs choose who sees when and where they practice.
