FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. No ads, no fees.

How do I play with my friends?

Tick them under Who's coming? when you create a game, or tap Reserve spots on a game you're in. Friends not on the app: tap Send invite link. They sign up and they're in.

Can I make a private game?

Yes. Under Who can join? pick Private. Invite friends right there, and use Share invite on the game page to send the link and password to anyone else.

What's team vs team?

You bring your team, another group challenges you. Pick it under Format when you create a game.

How do I add friends?

Tap the people icon at the top and search their name or UW NetID.

Will I get spammed?

No. Pick what you hear about in Settings → Notifications.

Who can see my email?

Only you, people you've played with, and officers of clubs you're a member of.

Does the app track my location?

No. "Where am I?" on a map stays on your phone.

Can't make it?

Tap Leave so someone else gets your spot. Hosts can cancel, and we email everyone.

Forgot your password?

Tap "Forgot password?" on the login page.

Change my photo, emails or delete my account?

Photo, bio and sports: Profile → Edit profile. Emails, password and deleting your account: Profile → Settings.

I run a club. What can I do here?

Register it (we check it's a real UW club), add your logo, post updates, set weekly practices in one go, make members-only events, confirm who joins, get a roster, and print a QR code for the involvement fair.

Got an idea or found a bug?

Send it in Suggestions.

Is this run by UW?

No, it's a student project. See Privacy and Terms.
