FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. No ads, no fees.

How do I play with my friends?

Tick them under Who's coming? when you create a game, or tap Reserve spots on a game you're in: their spot is held for 30 minutes while they say yes. If they don't answer in time, the spot opens up for anyone (their invite still works if there's room), and you can reserve it for them again. Friends not on the app: tap Send invite link. They sign up and they're in. Saw a public game a friend would like? Tap Send to friends to message it to them (nothing is held for them).

Who can I message?

Friends, people you've played a game with (once it's over), club officers, and anyone who wrote to you first. Everyone going to a game can talk in its 💬 group chat. Hold a message (or double-tap) to react; turn off Read receipts in Settings if you'd rather not show "Seen".

Can I make a private game?

Yes. Under Who can join? pick Private. Invite friends right there, or tap Send invite link on the game page: whoever opens it gets in without the password. The link doesn't show the place until they're in, so it's safe if it gets forwarded.

What's team vs team?

You bring your team, another group challenges you. Pick it under Format when you create a game.

How do I add friends?

Tap the people icon at the top and search their name or UW NetID.

Will I get spammed?

No. When a friend or a club you follow posts a game, you get one heads-up (at most 3 emails a day). Pick what you hear about in Settings → Notifications, and turn the emails off in Settings or with the Unsubscribe link at the bottom of any optional email (no login needed). A friend can hold you a spot in up to 5 games a day, and if a game you're in changes many times in one day, the extra changes show in your notifications instead of more emails.

Is there an app?

Put it on your home screen and it opens like one. iPhone: in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Android: tap Add when it's offered.

Who can see my email?

Only you, people you've played with, and officers of clubs you're a member of. Never someone you blocked or who blocked you.

Does the app track my location?

No. "Where am I?" on a map stays on your phone.

Can't make it?

Tap Leave so someone else gets your spot. Hosts can cancel, and we email everyone.

Forgot your password?

Tap "Forgot password?" on the login page.

Change my photo, emails or delete my account?

Photo, bio and sports: Profile → Edit profile. Emails, password and deleting your account: Profile → Settings.

I run a club. What can I do here?

Register it (we check it's a real UW club), add your logo, post updates, set weekly practices in one go, make members-only events, confirm who joins, get a roster, and print a QR code for the involvement fair. Pick your club's sport from the list (boxing, rugby, judo, water polo and many more). Whoever registers the club is its owner: on Manage officers they add or remove officers, and can make another officer the owner. Club events belong to the club: any officer can edit or cancel them. Someone who leaves the club (or is removed) is also taken out of its upcoming members-only events. Your club page is public, so for members-only and private events it only says there's a new event, not when or where.

Got an idea or found a bug?

Send it in Suggestions.

Is this run by UW?

No, it's a student project. See Privacy and Terms.
