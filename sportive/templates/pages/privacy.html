Privacy

Your data, in plain words

Last updated September 29, 2026

Sportive Circle is a student project that helps UW students find people to play sports with. It is not run by the University of Washington. We collect only what the app needs, we never sell it, and there are no ads.

What we collect

  • Your account: name, UW email, password (stored scrambled, never readable), date of birth (only to check you're old enough and to say happy birthday), and graduation year if you add it.
  • Club officers' phone numbers: asked when registering a club, seen only by our admins and the club's owner, to reach you about the request.
  • Your phone number, only if you add it for texts: used only to text you codes, the game updates you asked for and, if you run a club, club alerts, never shown to anyone and never sold. Turn texts off or remove it anytime in Settings → Texts, or reply STOP.
  • Your profile: photo, bio, the sports you like, and, only if you add them, pronouns, gender and social media usernames.
  • What you do in the app: games you host or join, badges, friends, clubs you follow or join (and your answer to a club's join question), messages and photos you send in chats, reactions, whether you've seen a message (shown as Seen unless you turn off Read receipts), notifications, and reports you send.
  • Security records: when a password is typed wrong, the device's internet address (IP) and the email typed, for about 15 minutes, to stop password guessing. And when we send codes, to limit them.

What we don't collect

  • Your location. "Where am I?" on a map runs only in your browser and is never sent to us.
  • Photo metadata. We remove hidden info (like where a photo was taken) when you upload it.
  • No tracking cookies and no advertising. The only cookie keeps you logged in.

Who can see what

  • Your name, photo, class year, bio, sports, badges, and any pronouns, gender or socials you add are visible to other logged-in UW students.
  • Your email is visible only to you, people you've played a game with, and the officers of clubs you're a member of (for the club's roster).
  • Direct messages are visible to you and the other person. Event chats are visible to everyone going to that event.
  • If you report something, a copy goes to our admins so they can review it. The person isn't told who reported them.
  • Suggestions you send are read only by our admins, with your name unless you choose "Send anonymously".
  • Admins can see account details when they need to handle a report or verify a club.

Other services the app uses

  • Email: codes, reminders and club notices are sent through our email provider.
  • Texts: if you turn them on, texts are sent through our texting provider (Twilio).
  • Maps: map images come from OpenStreetMap, and the map code from cdnjs. Your browser downloads them directly, so those services see your IP address, like any website you visit. Directions open in Google Maps or Apple Maps only if you tap them.
  • Fonts: one font comes from Google Fonts.

Your choices

  • Edit your profile, photo and sports any time in Profile → Edit profile. Email reminders and notifications are in Settings.
  • Delete your account in Settings. Your profile, photo, upcoming games you host, messages and badges are deleted right away. Past games you hosted that others played stay in their history under "Former member". Club updates and suggestions you posted stay without your name. Reports about other people stay for safety, without your name.
