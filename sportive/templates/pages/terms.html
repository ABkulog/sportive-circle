The rules of the pack

Last updated September 27, 2026

By using Sportive Circle you agree to these rules. They're short, so please read them.

Who can use it

  • UW students with a working UW email address, age 15 or older.
  • One account per person, with your real name and a photo of you.

Be a good teammate

  • No harassment, bullying, hate, threats or sexual content.
  • No spam, scams or selling things.
  • Don't pretend to be someone else, or register a club you aren't an officer of.
  • If you can't make a game, tap Leave. If you host, cancel the event when plans change.
  • Follow the UW Student Conduct Code when you meet up on campus.

We can remove content or suspend accounts that break these rules. If you think we got it wrong, {% if contact %}email {{ contact }}{% else %}contact an admin{% endif %}.

Playing safely

  • Sports can cause injuries. You play at your own risk and are responsible for your own safety and gear.
  • Sportive Circle doesn't reserve courts or fields. UW Recreation facilities like the IMA may require a UW Recreation membership or Husky Card, and their rules apply.
  • Meet in public places. If you ever feel unsafe, leave, and in an emergency call 911. UW Police's non-emergency line is 206-685-8973, and SafeCampus is 206-685-7233.

The fine print

  • Sportive Circle is a student project, not an official University of Washington service. UW names are used only to describe where and with whom you can play.
  • The app is provided as is. It may have bugs or downtime, and features may change.
  • What you post is yours. You let us show it in the app so it works (for example, your event on the feed).
  • See the Privacy page for how we handle your data.
