How it works

Find people to play sports with, in a few taps.

Here's everything, one step at a time.

  1. 1

    Sign up with your UW email

    Use your @uw.edu email. We send you a 6-digit code to make sure it's really you. That way, everyone on here is a real UW student.

  2. 2

    Add a profile picture

    Pick a clear photo of your face. It helps people find you when you show up to play.

  3. 3

    Pick the sports you like

    Basketball, soccer, climbing, running and more. You can change them any time in Profile → Edit profile.

  4. 4

    Look at your feed

    The Feed shows games for the sports you picked. Want to see everything? Tap All sports. You can also filter by sport, day, place and level.

  5. 5

    Tap “Join”

    Found a game you like? Tap Join. That's it, you're in. You'll see You're going on it.

  6. 6

    Nothing you like? Start your own

    There are two buttons at the bottom of the screen:

    ⚡ Need players

    For right now. “We have 8, we need 2 more for soccer.” It goes to the top of everyone's feed.

    + New event

    For planning ahead. “Sunday pickup soccer at 11.” Pick the day, time, place and how many people.

  7. 7

    Show up and play

    Not sure where it is? Every event has a map. Tap 🧭 Directions and your phone walks you there.

    We email you 1 hour before it starts, and your next game shows up at the top of your feed. You can also tap 📅 Add to calendar on any event.

  8. 8

    Can't make it? Tap “Leave”

    Plans change, no problem. Just tap Leave so someone else can take your spot. If you're the host, tap Cancel event so everyone knows.

  9. 9

    Bring your friends

    Tap 🔗 Share on any event and send it to your group chat. More people means more games!

Good to know

Is it free?

Yes, completely.

Who can see my email?

Only you, and people who go to the same game as you. Nobody else.

Do you track my location?

No. “Where am I?” only runs on your phone to show how far away you are. We never see or save your location.

What if a game is full?

You'll see Full and can't join. If someone leaves, a spot opens up again.

Why can't I pick some places for some sports?

We only show places where you can actually play that sport. No basketball on the running trail!

How do I stop the reminder emails?

Go to Profile → Edit profile and uncheck “Email me an hour before events.”

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile → Edit profile and scroll to the bottom.

Be a good teammate 🤝

Show up on time, be friendly to beginners, and tap Leave if you can't come.
