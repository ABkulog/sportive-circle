How it works

Play sports with Huskies in 5 steps.

  1. 🎓

    Sign up with your UW email

    A 6-digit code proves you're a Husky.

  2. 🏠

    Find a game

    Home shows games for your sports. Tap Join.

  3. +

    Or start one

    ⚡ Need players for right now, 📅 New event for later.

  4. 🏛️

    Join a club

    Follow verified UW clubs. Officers confirm new members.

  5. 🔥

    Rank up (if you want)

    Play, get 🤝 props and ⬆️ vouches to unlock harder games.

Good to know

How do ranks work?

Everyone starts at Casual in each sport. Rep from playing, plus vouches from teammates, moves you up to Intermediate, Competitive and Legend. Ranks never go down.

I'm already good. Do I have to grind?

No. Take a 🎟️ tryout spot in a higher game. If 3 good players vouch for you, you're placed in their tier.

Can I play with a friend who isn't ranked?

Yes. In an Intermediate or Competitive game, tap 🤝 Bring a friend to invite one friend as your +1.

I don't care about ranks.

Turn on 🧘 chill mode in your profile. Casual and All-levels games never show ranks anyway.

How do clubs work?

⭐ Follow a club to see its updates. To become a member, request to join, apply or try out. You're a member once an officer confirms you.

Can't make it to a game?

Tap 👋 Leave on the game so someone else gets your spot. Hosts: cancel it so everyone knows.

Is it free?

Yes, completely.

Who can message me?

Friends, people you've played with, and club officers. Tap ⋯ → Block or 🚩 Report on anyone who bothers you.

Who can see my email?

Only you and people you've played with.

Do you track my location?

No. "Where am I?" on a map only runs on your phone.

How do I turn off reminder emails or delete my account?

Profile → Edit profile. Both are there.
